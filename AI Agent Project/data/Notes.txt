Brihadeshwara temple is a Hindu temple dedicated to Lord Shiva. It is located in Thanjavur, Tamil Nadu, India. The temple was built during the Chola dynasty in the 11th century and is known for its magnificent architecture and intricate carvings. The temple is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and attracts thousands of visitors every year.
